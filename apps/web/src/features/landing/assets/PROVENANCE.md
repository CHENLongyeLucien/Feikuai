# Globe land geometry

`ne_110m_land.geojson` is the Natural Earth 1:110m land dataset, downloaded from the project's official repository:

- Data: https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_110m_land.geojson
- License: https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/LICENSE.md

Natural Earth data is in the public domain. The application triangulates the coastline polygons onto a procedural sphere. Ocean, relief, colors and lighting are presentation choices.

Transaction arcs are deterministic illustrative placements derived from transaction hashes. They do not use or imply sender/recipient geography. Avalanche transaction records do not provide that information.
